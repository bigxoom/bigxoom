# Restaurant & Bar POS Guide

1. Choose **Restaurant** or **Bar** at the top of the POS screen.
2. Enter the table number (or select a room for a guest charge).
3. Tap menu items to add them to the order.
4. Tap **Send to Kitchen** — the order appears instantly on the Kitchen
   Display, no refresh needed.
5. When the guest is ready to settle: pay directly, or charge to their room
   folio (charges appear immediately in Reception's view of that guest's
   folio).

Void/refund/discount actions are restricted to supervisor-approved roles and
are always recorded in the audit log with a reason.
