# Reception Guide

## Guest journey
Reservation → Confirmed → Check-in → Occupied → Check-out → Dirty → Cleaning
→ Ready.

## Check-in
Open **Reservations**, find the booking, click **Check in**. This creates
the guest's Stay and opens their Guest Folio, and sets the room to OCCUPIED.

## Charging services to a room
Any authorized department (restaurant, bar, laundry) posts charges directly
onto the guest's open folio via their own screen (e.g. "Charge to room" in
POS). Reception always sees the live, complete folio for a stay.

## Check-out
From the guest's folio, confirm the balance is fully paid (via Payments),
then check out. The system will refuse checkout if there is an outstanding
balance. On successful check-out the room automatically becomes DIRTY and a
housekeeping task is created — no manual step needed.

## Room map
The **Rooms** screen shows all 90 rooms colour-coded by status, refreshed
live.
