# Kitchen Display Guide

The Kitchen screen shows every active order in real time over the LAN
(WebSockets — no internet required, no page refresh needed).

Each ticket shows: table or room, items, quantities, and any notes.

Tap **Advance to next stage** to move a ticket through
NEW → ACCEPTED → PREPARING → READY → SERVED.
Every status change is timestamped and attributed to the staff member who
made it, and is visible to the waiter/bar staff and to Admin instantly.
